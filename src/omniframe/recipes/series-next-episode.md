# Series episode with a callback

## What it is
One episode of a series: a short recap of the last one, the new step, and a teaser for the next, in the same look and music.

## Fits when
The user has made earlier videos in this format, and the new one continues it.

## Hook, first 3 s
Open with the recap line over the same opening shot that the earlier episodes used. The title shows the episode number.

## Shot order
**15 s**
- 0-2 s: title with the episode number and the recap line.
- 2-9 s: the new step.
- 9-13 s: result.
- 13-15 s: teaser line.

**30 s**
- 0-4 s: title and recap.
- 4-14 s: step 1.
- 14-24 s: step 2.
- 24-27 s: result.
- 27-30 s: teaser.

**60 s**
- 0-6 s: title and recap.
- 6-22 s: step 1.
- 22-38 s: step 2.
- 38-48 s: step 3.
- 48-54 s: result.
- 54-60 s: teaser for the next episode.

## Captions and type
Use the caption and title styles of the earlier episodes. `studio_guide` lists the saved library entries, so reuse the ones that fit.

## Motion and transitions
Repeat the opening move and the end card, so viewers know the format. Change only the middle. Keep the transitions of the earlier episodes.

## Sound and music
The same theme, and the same sound for the title and the end card. New voice lines.

## Material needed
Earlier episodes' saved elements (library entries), new footage or graphics, and a new voiceover.

## Common mistakes
- A recap longer than a few seconds.
- A look that changes between episodes with no reason.
- A teaser that promises something the next episode does not show.

## OmniFrame tools to use
- `studio_guide`: the saved library and the user's preferences, at the start.
- `learn_and_record_memory`: save the series rules when the user asks.
- `render_video(mode="final")`, then `qa_verify_video`.
- `export_package`, with its library option for the elements the user agrees to keep.
